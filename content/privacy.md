## Overview

Apps made by Atheenea are designed with your privacy in mind. This page describes the general privacy approach for all apps. Individual apps may have their own supplemental privacy pages linked from their app page.

## Data I collect

Most Atheenea apps collect **no personal data** at all. Where an app does collect data (for example, to enable sync or crash reporting), it is described clearly in that app's privacy page.

## Third-party services

Some apps may use Apple's own services (CloudKit, StoreKit) for sync or purchases. These are governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

I do not use third-party advertising. Some apps may use analytics SDKs to help improve them in the future. Sharing analytics data is always opt-in.
