Applies to: NextBook 1.0

## Data collection

NextBook collects no personal data. There are no user accounts, no sign-ins, and no data sent to any server.

## What stays on your device

All data you enter (book titles, release dates, and notification preferences) is stored locally on your device using iOS system storage. It never leaves your device.

## Deleting your data

All data stored by NextBook can be permanently deleted 
by removing the app from your device.

## Notifications

NextBook uses iOS local notifications to remind you of release days. These are scheduled entirely on-device. No notification tokens or device identifiers are transmitted to any server.

## Third-party services

NextBook does not use any third-party SDKs, analytics tools, or advertising frameworks.

The one-time purchase of NextBook is handled by Apple's App Store and governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
