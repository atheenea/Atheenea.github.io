## Status

Aeva is currently in development. A full privacy policy will be published here before the app launches.

Atheenea's general commitment applies in the meantime: no personal data is collected beyond what is strictly necessary, no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs are used, and your data belongs to you.

## Research (future consideration)

Women remain underrepresented in health research. Better data from women's lived experiences could help change that.

Aeva may in the future offer users the option to contribute their anonymised data to women's health research. This would be:

- Entirely optional and based on explicit consent
- Fully anonymised before any sharing
- Clearly explained before you decide

You can withdraw consent at any time. Please note that once data has been fully anonymised and contributed to research, it may no longer be possible to remove it from that dataset.

This feature is not yet implemented. If and when it is introduced, this privacy policy will be updated accordingly and you will be informed.
